Subject: New in Outlook: "Known sender" and "Unknown sender" labels

Hi everyone,

Starting today, incoming email in Outlook shows one of two labels:

- ✓ (blue checkmark): a Known sender we have personally verified.
- Unknown sender (gray): not verified yet. Normal for new contacts; it does not mean the email is bad.

Three things to remember:

1. A Known sender is not a guarantee of safety. Real suppliers' mailboxes can be hacked.
2. Never change bank or payment details from an email alone. Call to confirm using a number you already have on file.
3. Watch for lookalike addresses (one letter different from a real supplier). Report anything suspicious with Outlook's Report button and tell {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}.

If a contact you trust shows Unknown sender, ask a registrar ({{REGISTRARS}}) to verify them.
